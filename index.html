ABOUT OUR DEMO BANK

Banking made simpler.

NorthBank is a sample banking brand created for this educational Bank Account Management System project.

Our Vision

To demonstrate a simple and convenient way to manage banking accounts through a clear digital interface.

Our Mission

To make account creation, deposits, withdrawals, transfers and transaction review easy to understand.

Our Services

  • Savings and current accounts
  • Cash deposits and withdrawals
  • Account-to-account transfers
  • Transaction history

Technology

HTML provides the page structure, CSS styles the interface, and JavaScript handles application logic and browser storage.

Contact Information

Email: support@northbank.example

Location: Tamil Nadu, India

Project Information

This is a front-end educational project. It is not connected to a real bank or payment network.

Important demo notice

Do not enter real personal or financial information. This application does not provide real authentication, bank-grade security or real-money transactions.
